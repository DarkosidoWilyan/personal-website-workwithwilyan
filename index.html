The Accounting Migration

ALAMI · Associate Product Manager complete

Situation
Oracle's accounting platform cost the company IDR 2–5 billion a year — finance needed a cheaper system without breaking a live, audited ledger.
Task
Migrate every financial record from Oracle to Jurnal before the Oracle contract expired, with full data integrity and zero disruption to daily accounting.
Action
Mapped every Oracle use case to its Jurnal equivalent, extracted and backed up the full historical dataset, ran weekly sync meetings to unblock engineering, verified migrated records line by line, wrote the integration requirements between the internal MINA system and Jurnal, and trained the finance team before cutover.
Result
100% of journal vouchers migrated and verified, the MINA–Jurnal integration shipped cleanly, and the team was fully live on Jurnal a month before the Oracle contract ended.
Role
Led end-to-end
Skills
  • Accounting Systems
  • Data Migration
  • Change Management
Impact
~IDR 1.6 billion saved in annual operating cost

Updating Agreement Document

ALAMI · Associate Product Manager complete

Situation
ALAMI's Funder umbrella agreement needed updating — new consumer data protection clauses, updated bondholder meeting terms, and other regulatory changes all had to be folded in.
Task
Get every Funder onto the new umbrella agreement without breaking their existing one or blocking anyone from signing.
Action
Wrote the requirements for the new agreement content and how Funders would access and sign it, ran a cost analysis for the rollout, mapped the downside risks and who was authorized to sign, released the new agreement in staged batches to limit blast radius, and built the Funder-facing communication explaining what changed and why they needed to re-sign.
Result
New Funders are fully on the updated agreement; most existing Funders are still on the old one while the team weighs trimming the content further to cut the cost of repeated signature rounds.
Role
Led
Skills
  • Umbrella Agreement
  • Requirements
  • Funder Communications

Onboarding Under New Regulation

ALAMI · Associate Product Manager complete

Situation
ALAMI had rolled out a new internal credit score, but Funders — who make the final financing call — hadn't been told what it meant for their exposure.
Task
Design and ship a notification flow that told Funders about the new credit scoring model and what would change for them.
Action
Wrote the requirements for how the new credit score would surface to Funders, tested the flow engineering built against it, and drafted the Funder-facing email announcing the change.
Result
Funders got clear, timely information about the credit score change, which kept complaints about it to a minimum.
Role
Led
Skills
  • Credit Scoring
  • Requirements
  • Funder Communications
Impact
Minimal Funder complaints after the change shipped
